Privacy Policy

We developed Kids Coloring as a free-to-use application for kids. This Privacy Policy explains how Kids Coloring deals with your information and how your data may be used over the Internet or on the Google Play Store.

The Kids Coloring app will never ask you for any of your personal information when you install or use this application. We do not require you to register as a customer or provide personal information to use the Kids Coloring app.

We do not operate a server to store your app data. Information created or used by the app is stored locally on your device. Kids Coloring does not track your information after you uninstall the application.

Cookies

Cookies are files containing a small amount of data that are commonly used as anonymous identifiers for devices. They may help applications improve functionality, navigation, and the user experience.

You may be able to reset your device or application settings to refuse cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. If you choose to refuse cookies, some portions of the service may not function as intended.

Advertisement

Kids Coloring uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Our advertising provider may use cookies, advertising identifiers, or similar technologies to recognize a device and provide advertisements that may be relevant to the application or its users.

We do not directly share users' personal information with social media platforms or other websites for commercial purposes.

Log File

The only situation in which Kids Coloring may receive personal information is when you voluntarily contact us by email, for example, to provide feedback or report a bug.

In such circumstances, the information we may receive can include your name, email address, device information, location information, and the contents of your message or bug report.

How We Use Your Information

If you voluntarily provide us with information when contacting us, we use that information only for responding to your request, investigating reported problems, and improving our services.

We will not intentionally use information such as your name or email address for unrelated marketing purposes or disclose it to third parties for commercial gain.
